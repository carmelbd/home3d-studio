How it works

Three steps, a few minutes.

  1. Draw the roomsStart empty or from the example home. Drag out each room and set its size.
  2. Furnish and planAdd furniture, doors and windows, then the lights and smart devices you want.
  3. Look around in 3DSwitch to 3D, walk through it, and save the design file to keep or share.

Have Home Assistant?

Install the free Home 3D card through HACS and your plan becomes a live dashboard: tap a lamp in 3D to turn it on, see who's home, watch the cameras.
